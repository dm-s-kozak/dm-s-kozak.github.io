Lab Fly

Lab Fly is a small experimental layer inside smysloTalk.

It is not another profile and not a map of the website. The public profile keeps the traces; the Biosketch-Graph keeps the relations. Lab Fly stays beside them as a small laboratory mode: one moving operator enters three situations — matter, field, and mistake.

The order is simple: first, a trace can be followed through material things; then, an event can be sensed through field responses; finally, a miss or error can expose the limit of recognition. That limit opens the question: whOwhat appeared?

Materials When something leaves a visible trace through matter, surfaces, samples, or handling. Fields When an event is sensed by its responses: discharge, impulse, light, sound, or field effects. Miss / error When a wrong move becomes a moment of distinction — and the question appears.
