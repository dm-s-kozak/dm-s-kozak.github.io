What this graph shows

This Biosketch-Graph is not a full biography and not a fixed hierarchy. It is a structured map of how different technical, experimental, and research layers connect into one developing academic-professional profile.

The central idea is simple: earlier materials and surface-engineering experience formed a technical foundation; this foundation entered the BioTeC+ / KU Leuven experimental research orbit; and it now develops into a profile connecting functional materials, field-assisted processing, antimicrobial and bio-related testing, setup work, and AI-assisted research structuring.

Reading logic

  • Foundation: what existed before KU Leuven.
  • Orbit: where the profile became academically anchored.
  • Current profile: what is active now.
  • Emerging directions: what is being carefully developed.
