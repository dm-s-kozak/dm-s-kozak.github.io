Profile

I work across at BioTec+ / KU Leuven experimental research, materials development, surface-related technologies, plasma/RF/PEF systems, antimicrobial testing, and early-stage R&D workflows.

My practical approach is based on minimal viable corridors: small, testable pathways through complex systems that can be expanded when they begin to hold.

AI-Assisted R&D

smysloTalk

smysloTalk is an exploratory project on trace-based reasoning, emergence tracking, and human–AI dialogue.

The name combines “smyslo” — from the Slavic root for meaning or sense — and “Talk”, pointing to dialogue as a medium where emerging ideas become traceable.
