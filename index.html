Selected Research Profile, 2020–present

This profile focuses on my selected research and experimental R&D trajectory from 2020 to the present. It is not a complete archive of my life or work, but a structured projection of the domains, traces, and working directions that shape my current profile.

During this period, my work has developed across functional materials, nanomaterial synthesis, surface-related technologies, plasma/RF/PEF systems, antimicrobial testing, and early-stage experimental R&D workflows.

I currently work at BioTeC+ / KU Leuven, where my experience connects laboratory experimentation, materials development, field-assisted methods, and AI-assisted research structuring.

My practical approach is based on minimal viable corridors: small, testable pathways through complex systems that can be expanded when they begin to hold.

Research & Project Areas

Physical / Field-Assisted methods

smysloTalk

smysloTalk is an exploratory project on trace-based reasoning, emergence tracking, and human–AI dialogue.

The name combines “smyslo” — from the Slavic root for meaning or sense — and “Talk”, pointing to dialogue as a medium where emerging ideas become traceable.
