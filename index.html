Selected Research Profile, 2020–present

My practical approach is based on minimal viable corridors: small, testable pathways through complex laboratory systems that can be expanded when they begin to hold.

Earlier Engineering and Postdoctoral Research Foundation

My current BioTeC+ / KU Leuven profile is grounded in an earlier engineering and postdoctoral research foundation. This background includes hands-on industrial-engineering experience with prototype parts, manufacturing-side coordination, transfer of components to working test stands, troubleshooting, documented testing traces, and international postdoctoral research experience.

Part of this earlier background is now integrated into my current experimental R&D profile: functional materials, setup development, field-assisted methods, prototype-level workflows, and research-enabling experimental traces.

This layer is presented not as a complete career history, but as a foundation that informs how I approach laboratory systems, material/process development, and the transition from idea to testable experimental setup.

smysloTalk

smysloTalk is an exploratory project on trace-based reasoning, emergence tracking, and human–AI dialogue.

The name combines “smyslo” — from the Slavic root for meaning or sense — and “Talk”, pointing to dialogue as a medium where emerging ideas become traceable.
